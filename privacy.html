OMI - Privacy policy

Last updated: October 2026.

OMI is a personal assistant used only by its owner, on the owner's own computer. This policy describes what OMI does with the Google data the owner chooses to connect.

What OMI accesses

  • Google Calendar (calendar.events): reads the owner's events and adds an event when the owner approves.
  • Gmail (gmail.readonly): searches and reads the owner's messages when the owner asks.
  • Gmail (gmail.send): sends an email only after the owner approves that exact email.

OMI does not access contacts, Google Drive or any other Google data, and it does not delete emails or events.

How the data is used

Calendar and email data is used only to answer the owner's requests (for example "what is on my calendar tomorrow") and to show the owner a morning summary and reminders before appointments. It is not used for advertising, not sold, not shared with third parties and not used to train AI models.

To answer a request, the text of the relevant events or emails may be sent to the AI model provider the owner has configured for OMI, only for that request.

Where it is stored

OMI runs on the owner's computer. The Google access grant is stored on that computer, encrypted for the owner's Windows account. OMI does not run any server that stores the owner's Google data.

Control and deletion

The owner can disconnect Google at any time in OMI's Settings, and can remove OMI's access in their Google Account under "Third-party apps with account access". Disconnecting deletes the stored grant.

Google API Services User Data Policy

OMI's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
