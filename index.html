OMI

OMI is a personal AI assistant that runs on its owner's own Windows PC. It helps its owner with their computer, reminders, calendar and email.

OMI is a private, single-owner application. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

When its owner connects Google, OMI can read the owner's Google Calendar events, read the owner's Gmail, add calendar events and send emails. Adding an event and sending an email always wait for the owner's approval.
